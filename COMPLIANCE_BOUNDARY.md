# COMPLIANCE_BOUNDARY.md

## PARRALAX-AIHFTFUND Compliance Boundary

### Version 1.0

---

## 1. Purpose

This document defines the regulatory and compliance boundaries within which PARRALAX-AIHFTFUND operates. It establishes what the system does and does not do from a legal and regulatory perspective.

---

## 2. What PARRALAX-AIHFTFUND IS

- A sovereign financial infrastructure platform
- A tool for the operator's own trading and fund management
- An agent-driven execution framework
- A digital asset management system
- A protocol-governed financial operating environment

---

## 3. What PARRALAX-AIHFTFUND IS NOT

- NOT a registered investment adviser
- NOT a broker-dealer (unless specifically licensed)
- NOT a money transmitter
- NOT a public offering of securities
- NOT financial advice to third parties
- NOT a custodian of client assets (operates on operator's own capital)

---

## 4. Regulatory Framework

### United States
- SEC: No public securities offering without compliance
- CFTC: Futures/derivatives trading subject to applicable rules
- FinCEN: AML/KYC requirements for money transmission
- State: Wyoming DAO LLC or equivalent if applicable

### International
- Comply with local securities regulations per jurisdiction
- No operations in sanctioned jurisdictions
- GDPR compliance for EU data subjects

---

## 5. Self-Imposed Boundaries

| Boundary | Rule |
|----------|------|
| Client Funds | System does NOT manage third-party funds without explicit licensing |
| Public Tokens | Internal tokens are NOT offered to the public without SEC compliance |
| Advisory | System does NOT provide investment advice to others |
| Custody | Operator maintains own custody — no custodial services to third parties |
| Reporting | Tax reporting obligations met per jurisdiction |

---

## 6. Compliance Controls

- All trades logged for tax reporting
- Transaction monitoring for suspicious activity
- Sanctions screening for counterparties
- Record retention per regulatory requirements (minimum 5 years)
- Annual compliance review

---

## 7. Licensing Path

If PARRALAX-AIHFTFUND expands to serve external participants:
1. Legal counsel engagement required
2. Appropriate registrations/licenses obtained
3. Compliance infrastructure implemented
4. Audit framework established
5. Insurance coverage secured

---

## 8. Disclaimer

This system is built for sovereign fund operators managing their own capital. Any expansion beyond self-directed operations requires appropriate legal and regulatory compliance. This document does not constitute legal advice.
